The Master Antioxidant

What is glutathione?

Glutathione is an antioxidant naturally produced by the body. It is found in every cell and helps protect against oxidative stress, supports detoxification, and contributes to normal immune system function.

What is glutathione made of?

Glutathione is made from three amino acids: cysteine, glycine, and glutamic acid. These are the building blocks the body needs to produce glutathione inside its cells.

How is it different from other vitamins?

Vitamins come from the diet and support specific functions in the body. Glutathione is produced within the body and acts at the cellular level, where it helps protect cells and plays a role in antioxidant processes.

Where is it found in the body?

Glutathione is present in cells throughout the body. Especially high levels are found in the liver, immune system, brain, lungs, and kidneys.

What happens when levels are low?

Low levels may make it harder for the body to manage stress and handle toxins. Some people may feel more exhausted or fatigued, or take longer to recover. Consult a healthcare professional if you have concerns.

A History of Excellence

  1. 1965

    An Idea Takes Shape

    Immunotec's journey began in the United States, where Dr. Gustavo Bounous began examining recovery in post-surgical patients. He continued this research in Canada to discover which nutritional elements might affect healing and recovery. A protein emerged as the most likely candidate.

  2. 1978

    Research Begins

    At McGill University, Dr. Bounous met Dr. Patricia Kongshavn, an emerging PhD researcher playing a pioneering role in the new field of clinical immunology. Together they published their first joint paper, “The Effect of Dietary Amino Acids on Immune Reactivity,” beginning their search for a protein that could improve immune function.

  3. 1981

    A Breakthrough

    Although the early trials did not produce revolutionary results, a protein extract was mailed to Dr. Bounous in the early 1980s for further study. When tested in animals, the extract showed notable results, including a 30% to 50% increase in life span.

  4. 1986

    Great Minds Think Alike

    During this period, Dr. Bounous learned of Dr. Wulf Droge's research, the first to describe glutathione's effect on the immune response of a living organism. Dr. Bounous realized his protein might affect glutathione levels. In 2005, Dr. Droge joined Immunotec as Senior Vice President of Research and Development.

  5. 1988

    New Beginnings

    With a strong medical foundation and a growing understanding of the protein's potential impact on glutathione and immune health, Dr. Bounous and Dr. Kongshavn launched Immunotec Research Corp with Dieter Beer to advance their research.

  6. 1993

    Science-Backed Growth

    The Montreal General Hospital team, working with McGill researchers, examined the protein's effects in patients with advanced HIV/AIDS following a rigorous review process. Dr. Bounous's team documented weight gain and increased glutathione levels. Although it was a small trial, it helped secure funding for further research.

  7. 1996

    A Product Goes Public

    Following this success, Immunotec officially launched in Canada in 1996 and in the United States in 1997.
